    Frequently asked questions

    Morph brings document, PDF, image, media, and data conversion into one focused workspace. Use it for a single file or a mixed batch, with an output choice for every file.

    What can I convert?

    You can convert documents, slides, images, audio, video, spreadsheets, and data. Check the table above to see all supported formats.

    Are my files private?

    Yes. Everything converts right on your device. Your files are never uploaded or sent to any server.

    Why is the first conversion a bit slower?

    The app loads the conversion tools it needs the first time you use them. After that, conversions are fast.

    Why do some files look slightly different?

    Some files use special fonts or styling that may not be available on your device, so they can look a little different.

    How do I convert multiple files?

    Pick a target format for each file, select the ones you want, and click convert. You can download them one by one or together in a ZIP file.
